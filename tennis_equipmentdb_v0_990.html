Kimony
About Kimony: Japanese grip manufacturer producing all grips in Japan (except the Tourna KGT141, made in USA). Renowned for their Hi-Soft polyurethane formula – widely considered the tackiest overgrip material available. The lineup spans wet/tacky overgrips (smooth, perforated, spiral-cushioned, textured), dry-feel overgrips for heavy sweaters, and premium replacement grips in both leather and synthetic. Hi-Soft EX is the flagship wet grip; Last Dry is the flagship dry grip.

Yonex
About Yonex: The world's largest racquet sports manufacturer, producing grips in Indonesia with materials from Japan. The Super Grap line (launched 1987) is one of the best-selling overgrips globally – enough tape sold to wrap around the world six times. The lineup spans wet/tacky overgrips (smooth, cushion-ridged, antibacterial), dry-feel overgrips, and replacement grips in both leather and synthetic. Wet Super Grap (AC102) is the flagship overgrip; Ultimum Leather is the premium replacement grip.

About Tourna: Tourna built its reputation on the original blue dry overgrip — the most copied grip in tennis. Tourna Grip Original (XL-1) is the reference for sweat absorption (though with intentionally low durability — typical pro changes it every match). Tac and Mega Tac are the wet/tacky alternatives. Pro Tour Leather is the leather replacement grip, slightly more textured than Fairway Leather. For comparison: Kimony Last Dry has subtle tackiness and 3× the durability of Tourna Original; Yonex Dry Grap is similar to Tourna but slightly less absorbent.
