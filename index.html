🎻 Natural Gut / Poly Hybrids

Natural gut is made from cow serosa fibers — the most elastic, comfortable, and powerful string material available. In a hybrid with polyester, gut provides feel, power, and arm protection while poly adds control and spin. The two dominant gut strings are functionally similar; choose based on availability and price.

⚠️ Cost & durability: Gut is expensive (~€40–55/set) and breaks faster than poly, especially in mains. Round, slick poly crosses extend gut life significantly. Shaped poly crosses (square, hex) provide more spin but saw into the gut. The Regna’s 16×19 pattern is more forgiving on gut than denser patterns. A good frame to start experimenting with gut hybrids.

The classic configuration — gut mains deliver power, comfort, and legendary feel while poly crosses add control, spin, and durability. The Regna’s open 16×19 pattern allows good string movement, so even round poly crosses provide decent spin. Gut mains + round poly crosses is the safest starting point.

🎻 Natural Gut / Poly Hybrids

Natural gut is made from cow serosa fibers — the most elastic, comfortable, and powerful string material available. In a hybrid with polyester, gut provides feel, power, and arm protection while poly adds control and spin. The two dominant gut strings are functionally similar; choose based on availability and price.

⚠️ Cost & durability: Gut is expensive (~€40–55/set) and breaks faster than poly, especially in mains. Round, slick poly crosses extend gut life significantly. Shaped poly crosses (square, hex) provide more spin but saw into the gut. The Percept’s 18×19 pattern and 66 RA stiffer frame benefit more from gut’s comfort properties. Shaped poly crosses are more valuable here for spin access.

Gut mains inject power and comfort into the Percept’s denser 18×19 stringbed. The stiffer 66 RA frame benefits more from gut’s natural dampening than the Regna does. Shaped poly crosses are more valuable here since the denser pattern resists spin.

🎻 Natural Gut / Poly Hybrids

Natural gut is made from cow serosa fibers — the most elastic, comfortable, and powerful string material available. In a hybrid with polyester, gut provides feel, power, and arm protection while poly adds control and spin. The two dominant gut strings are functionally similar; choose based on availability and price.

⚠️ Cost & durability: Gut is expensive (~€40–55/set) and breaks faster than poly, especially in mains. Round, slick poly crosses extend gut life significantly. Shaped poly crosses (square, hex) provide more spin but saw into the gut. The PT630 is the classic gut hybrid frame — Kuerten, Murray, and Muster all used gut in this mold. The low power and dense 18×20 make it the ideal candidate.

The PT630 is arguably the single best frame for a gut hybrid. The 58 RA flex needs power injection that gut provides, the dense 18×20 needs the elasticity gut adds to a dead stringbed, and the Twaron dampening combines with gut’s natural comfort for legendary feel. Kuerten won the 1997 French Open with gut in this frame.
